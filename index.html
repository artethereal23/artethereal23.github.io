    02 — About

    Trained in fine art. Driven by curiosity. Happiest with paint on my hands.

    I'm Prakruti, a multidisciplinary visual artist working across drawing, painting, murals and commissioned artwork.

    My foundation is a Bachelor's degree in Visual Arts, grounded in drawing, painting, composition and art history. Postgraduate studies at Seneca College, including a full course in life drawing, sharpened my eye for the figure, light and form.

    For more than eight years I’ve painted murals for cafés, restaurants, offices, homes and IIM Ahmedabad, and hand-painted furniture, planters and bottles for private clients. Every new surface teaches me something, and I adapt quickly to new mediums and scales.

    Alongside my own practice, I share what I've learned through teaching and mentoring. I'm open to murals, commissions, collaborations and workshops.

    1. Bachelor of Visual ArtsDrawing, painting, composition, art history

    Artist statement

    I believe art should be felt, lived with and shared. My practice moves from a quick sketch to a finished wall, always rooted in drawing, colour and close observation, and always learning something new.

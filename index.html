02 — About

Trained in fine art. Fluent in pixels. Happiest with paint on my hands.

I'm Prakruti, a visual artist working across drawing, painting, digital illustration and murals.

My foundation is a Bachelor's degree in Visual Arts. I went on to postgraduate studies in 3D Animation and Visual Effects at Seneca College, where I learned to think in light, form, and story as well as line and colour. Today my work moves easily between a sketchbook, a canvas, a screen and a wall.

For more than eight years I’ve painted murals for cafés, restaurants, offices, homes and IIM Ahmedabad, and hand-painted furniture, planters and bottles for private clients.

Along the way I found another love: teaching. As a Student Peer Mentor at Seneca, I supported a group of around ten students. I've also assisted children's art classes, helping young artists find the confidence to pick up a brush and trust their own ideas.

I'm now building my body of work and seeking murals, commissions, creative-studio roles and teaching opportunities. If you have a wall, a project, or a classroom in mind, I'd love to hear about it.
